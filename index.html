How the numbers are worked out
  • Installment = chit amount ÷ members.
  • Total bid includes the foreman fee. Dividend per member = total bid ÷ members.
  • Each member pays = installment − dividend per member.
  • Winner gets = chit amount − total bid.
  • Your profit = what you receive − everything you pay over the full term.
  • Total gain = profit + interest on the money you receive, from the month you receive it to the last month.
  • RD = your monthly payments deposited each month at the RD rate (quarterly compounding), valued at the end of the term.

Example: ₹10,00,000, 20 members, 3% fee. Dividend ₹11,500 (total bid ₹2,30,000) in month 1: each member pays ₹38,500 and the winner gets ₹7,70,000.
